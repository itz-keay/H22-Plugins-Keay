Pure greys (#808080 and friends) look flat on screen. Add a tiny bit of warmth or coolness to every neutral so they feel intentional.

## My recipe

1. Pick one hue for your neutrals and keep saturation between 2–8%.
2. Create 9–11 steps from near-black to near-white.
3. Choose **one** accent color and use it sparingly: links, primary buttons, highlights.
4. Check contrast: body text should reach at least 4.5:1 against its background.
