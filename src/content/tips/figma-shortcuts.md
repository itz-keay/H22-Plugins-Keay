---
title: 10 Figma shortcuts I use every day
summary: "Small habits that save hours over a project."
date: 2026-06-03
tag: Workflow
---

- `Shift + A`: add auto layout
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Alt + K`: create component
- `Ctrl/Cmd + D`: duplicate
- `Shift + R`: toggle rulers
- `Alt` + hover: measure distances
- `Ctrl/Cmd + /`: quick actions
- `Shift + 2`: zoom to selection
- `Ctrl/Cmd + G`: group
- `Ctrl/Cmd + Alt + G`: frame selection
- `Shift + H` / `Shift + V`: flip
