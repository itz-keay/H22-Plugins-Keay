## The brief

Lumen needed an identity that felt human and optimistic in a category full of cold blues and stock photos of panels.

## The idea

The mark is a simple sunrise built from two arcs. It works as a logo, a loading animation and a pattern. A warm orange carries the brand; neutral greys keep it grown-up.

## Outcome

- Logo suite, color and type system
- 40-page brand guidelines
- Launch website and social templates
