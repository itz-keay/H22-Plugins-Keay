## Problem

Support tickets showed that most users opened the app just to ask "where is my parcel?", and couldn't find the answer.

## Process

Interviews, a card sort, three rounds of prototypes and a design system to hand off to engineering.

## Result

A single, live tracking card became the home screen. Support tickets dropped and ratings went up.
